Brand & Product Design — Crewnox Innovation Labs

Crewnox

Every studio sells someone else’s story. This time, we had to tell our own.

Crewnox had built a strong body of client work, but its own website hadn’t kept pace: it was the one project the studio kept putting last. This is the story of rebuilding that site, from a blank canvas to a screen ready to hand to developers.

The ask
Elevate the brand experience of the Crewnox website
The territory
Homepage, portfolio system, enquiry flow, theming
The finish line
Development‑ready UI, light & dark

01 — Overview

A studio’s site is its first pitch.

Before a prospective client ever talks to Crewnox, they scroll through its website. The brief was simple to state and hard to deliver: make that scroll feel like proof the studio can be trusted with someone else’s brand.

The problem

The studio’s portfolio had to carry a lot at once — service breadth, case‑study depth, and a route to enquiry — without ever feeling like a generic template. It needed a structure that could hold many projects across many industries, and still read as considered rather than crowded.

The approach

Work moved through three stages inside one Figma file: an open exploration phase to test layout and tone, a resource library to keep imagery and moodboards consistent, and a development‑ready stage where every flow — including edge cases like empty categories — was finalised for handoff.

What shipped

  • Homepage in light and dark theme
  • Sub‑category landing pages per service
  • “All Projects” filterable portfolio grid
  • Enquiry form with confirmation state
  • Blank state for categories with no live work
  • Full mobile‑responsive pass across every screen

The whole site was designed around one intended path — get someone from curiosity to a submitted enquiry with as little friction as possible.

User path

  1. 01Homepage

    Featured projects, services, curated categories

  2. 02Sub‑category or All Projects

    Browse by specialism or the full case‑study grid

  3. 03Project review

    Case studies build the case for getting in touch

  4. 04Enquiry form

    Frictionless capture of project details

  5. 05Confirmation

    Clear next step, no dead ends

02 — Design

Finding the shape of the page before finishing it.

The design phase was deliberately messy.

Hero variations

Four distinct hero directions were built out in full.

Portfolio grid density

The “All Projects” and featured‑work sections were tested at four, five, six, seven, and eight‑card densities.

Theme & gradient exploration

A dedicated gradient study fed the light and dark theme system.

Responsive drafts

Mobile versions of the homepage and portfolio pages were drafted in parallel with desktop.

05 — Outcome

What the redesign actually changes.

A consistent brand system

Light and dark themes, hero treatments, and grid formats all come from one shared resource library.

A shorter path to enquiry

Every route, from homepage, category, or full grid, leads to the same low‑friction enquiry form.

Responsive by design

Mobile layouts were drafted alongside desktop, across both themes, from the start.

No dead ends

Categories with no published work get a blank state that keeps visitors exploring.
