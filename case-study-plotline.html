Plotline

A planning tool for small creative teams that makes the shape of a project visible at a glance.

Type
Digital product design
Timeline
10 weeks (concept)
Role
Discovery, interaction design, design system
Shipped
Core views, component system, prototype

This is a fictional concept project. There is no real client, and nothing here describes real results.

The challenge

Small creative teams outgrow spreadsheets but find project tools heavy, with dozens of views and settings they never use. Plotline needed to show the shape of the work without turning into another dashboard.

The approach

Fewer views, each with a clear job: a timeline for the whole, a board for the week, a page for each project. A compact component system keeps dense screens readable on desktop and phone alike.

The process

Four steps, one thread.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We looked at how small teams actually plan: on whiteboards, in chat threads and in spreadsheets. The common thread was needing to see overlap, where two things want the same person at the same time.

    Output: research summary and opportunity areas

  2. 02

    Define

    We limited the product to three views and wrote a rule for each: what it shows, what it hides and when you would leave it.

    Output: product principles and information architecture

  3. 03

    Design

    Interaction first: dragging, resizing and reassigning on the timeline. Then a small component system (tokens, cards, rows, chips) so every screen was built from the same parts.

    Output: design system and core screens

  4. 04

    Deliver

    A prototype of the timeline-to-board-to-detail flow, plus component documentation with usage and accessibility notes.

    Output: prototype and system documentation

What shipped

Screens shipped

The core views, on desktop and phone.

What we took from it

Constraint is a feature

Saying no to extra views is what keeps the product readable.

System first, screens second

A small component set made new screens quick and consistent.

Plan for density

Dense screens only work when spacing and type are tightly specified.
